CONTACT

One inbox, read by one person.

There is no team and no support desk. Everything below reaches the same place — say which it is in the subject line and it gets to the right pile faster.

devan@explorationeconomy.org

What to send, and where it goes first

A finding against the work

The most valuable thing you can send. Name the artifact — file plus JSON key — so it can be checked in minutes. Start from the replication kit; the claims register maps every headline number to the artifact behind it. Different numbers on your hardware is a finding, not a nuisance.

Review enquiries and engagement terms

Economists, security researchers, identity and mechanism-design people. Reviews are hosted unedited, criticisms included — see the ones already published. Say what access you need and what you would want in return.

Supporting the work

Funding, introductions, or hands. What the money would do is written down before anyone gives any.

Press, talks, and everything else

Same address. If you are writing about the project, the independent reviews and the failure log are the least flattering and most accurate places to start.

What to expect. This is one person, so replies are not instant — but findings get answered. There is a standing 30-day verify-and-correct window before findings are treated as public record: no gag, nothing to remediate, just time to fix the record before it is cited.

Prefer to follow along rather than write? The list is free and infrequent — subscribe here. Everything published, including the failures, goes out the same way.
