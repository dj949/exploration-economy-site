The Power of Stories and How They Can Save Us

Everything humanity has ever built — or destroyed — began as a story. So did the way out.

Stories inspire, teach, divide, and unite us. They become the beliefs that shape everything that follows — whether they're true or not. Money, nations, and economies are stories too: powerful precisely because we all keep telling them. Which means the way out of a broken story isn't a war. It's a better story, told well, opted into.

Why We Feel Powerless — And What You Can Do About It
