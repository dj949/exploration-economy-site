WORKING BLUEPRINT RELEASE 1 JULY 2026

Money that begins with creation, not debt.

Nearly every dollar in circulation was born as a loan, and new money settles on whoever already owns assets. This is a working blueprint for the alternative: an economy that mints value from verified human creativity — where your data, your work, and your attention pay you when they're used, and AI pays the people it learns from.

Nothing here is for sale. No token, no coin offering. Your dollars keep working exactly as they do today.

SHEET 01Where money comes from

Almost every dollar is born as someone's debt.

Not a conspiracy — a design, documented in every economics textbook: when a bank issues a loan, new money comes into existence. Three consequences follow from that one choice:

CONSEQUENCE 1

Money is loaned into being

Nearly every new dollar is created when a bank lends and arrives owing interest. That supports real credit — but who the new purchasing power reaches is decided by who can borrow.

CONSEQUENCE 2

Assets earn; time doesn't

New money flows toward what's already owned — property, stock, capital. Offer only your time and effort, and you fall behind by design. Five generations on, 79% of the same families are still on top.

CONSEQUENCE 3

Creativity is treated as free raw material

Everything you write, post, rate, and share is collected, aggregated, sold — and now used to train AI — while the people who made it are paid nothing.

None of this needs a villain. It's a design — and designs can be revised. The only interesting question is whether a revision survives honest testing.

SHEET 02The proposed mechanism

Mint from creation. Make machines pay.

The revision under test: an optional layer on the internet we already have, where new money is created exactly one way — a real, verified human genuinely engaging with real work. Your data, creations, and attention become assets that pay you when used.

RULE 0 — THE ONE RULE EVERYTHING ELSE DEFENDS

Humans mint. Machines pay. An AI never creates money — when it learns from your work or acts on someone's behalf, it buys and pays existing units to the people whose work it used. The more capable AI becomes, the more it pays the humans it learned from. The money supply stays tied to the one thing no server farm can fake: verified human time.

Reviews can't be quietly buried — and buying them doesn't pay — so honest, verifiable work simply earns more; in the century-scale model, corruption ran out of places to hide rather than being policed away — transparent markets captured 96% of modeled sales on their own.modeled: 96% market share, no enforcement

Wealth and success stay fully possible — they just stop being automatic. Income streams can't be purchased or passed down, so staying on top means continuing to create real value. Internally, the reference design is codenamed EDEN (Exploration-Driven Economic Network) and its unit EVE (Equitable Value Exchange). These are working titles, held as loosely as every claim here — if a name ever gets between the idea and the people it's for, the name loses. The commitment is the mechanism: an exploration-driven economy.

SHEET 03Failure log — published, not buried

We defined failure first. Then we caused it three times.

Registered before any test ran: if the poorest tenth can't afford essentials for three months running, the design fails. Months were then spent trying to make exactly that happen. Three crashes, three redesigns — original wreckage archived beside every fix.

FAILURE REPORT 01 — "THE BATHTUB"RETEST: PASS
OBSERVED
Prices climbed ~10% a year; the bottom tenth went under by month 16 of the very first simulation — and never recovered.
ROOT CAUSE
A faucet that never turns off (every hour of attention mints coins) draining into a nearly closed drain.
CORRECTIVE ACTION
A thermostat, not a chairman: a formula in the constitution slows minting whenever money outruns real goods.
RETEST RESULT
The modeled price index held inside the registered storm envelope through fifteen simulated years; index governance and oracle integrity (C-12/C-13) remain open.
FAILURE REPORT 02 — "THE CLIFF"RETEST: PASS
OBSERVED
Half of lower earners rationally stopped working; output fell by a quarter.
ROOT CAUSE
The generous first safety net "topped everyone up" to the same line — a musician earning 70% of it gained nothing by working.
CORRECTIVE ACTION
The ramp: every coin earned adds half a coin above the guarantee, always — effort is never pointless.
RETEST RESULT
The simulated economy kept essentially all of its output.
FAILURE REPORT 03 — "THE HEIST"RETEST: PASS
OBSERVED
An optimizing fraud ring earned 6× the floor per fake identity; rings rotating AI-generated content could capture a third of all new money.
ROOT CAUSE
The first fraud tests had only imagined lazy thieves. An adversarial audit imagined smart ones.
CORRECTIVE ACTION
Three locks: near-identical content shares one shrinking payout; earning at scale requires a stake you lose if caught; your one lifetime identity is too valuable to rent out.
RETEST RESULT
Re-run against all three locks, the heist didn't pay in a single tested case.

SHEET 04Open items — what simulation cannot settle

Two hinges hold all of this up. Neither is proven.

Simulation took the design as far as simulation can go. What remains are questions only the real world can answer — listed here the way we track them, with failure defined in advance.

H-1
Will institutions actually pay for data and code?The economics only work if real buyers show up. A field experiment is designed and registered — with the result that would kill the idea written down first.
OPEN — EXPERIMENT DESIGNED
H-2
Does one-person-one-identity hold against real adversaries?Everything rests on verified humans staying verified. Only a contained, live pilot can answer it; the simulated defenses are not the proof.
OPEN — PILOT DESIGNED
F-1
The income floorIt didn't fund itself under stress-testing, so it is not promised. Instead: a fully tested blueprint any community or government can fund and run in public — modeled at roughly a quarter cheaper than traditional welfare delivery.
BLUEPRINT — NOT A PROMISE

If either hinge fails, this project will say so and publish the wreckage — that's the operating rule everything above was built under.

SHEET 05Build sequence

Nothing irreversible gets built on an unproven foundation.

Deliberately sequenced: each phase earns the next, and no phase requires believing in the last one.

PHASE 1 — SHIPPED

Your data improves your own life

EdenQuest — a free personal-growth app: the habit of your data serving you first. The first working part, useful even if nothing else is ever built.

PHASE 2 — IN PROGRESS

Ship the work; invite hostile review

Essays, white paper, replication kit — into the hands of outside economists and independent, out-of-family replicators. The review this project openly hasn't had yet.

PHASE 3 — DESIGNED

Run the two experiments (H-1, H-2)

Will institutions pay? Does identity hold? Field experiment and contained pilot, both with failure defined in advance.

PHASE 4 — BLUEPRINT

The floor, switched on by whoever funds it

A reserve you can watch fill, activation by formula, an exit that strands no one. A progress bar, not a pledge.
