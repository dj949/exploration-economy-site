THE BIGGER PICTURE

The first rung of a much taller ladder.

EdenQuest stands entirely on its own — a good app, worth using for its own sake. It is also, deliberately, the first step into the Exploration Economy. Here's the whole ladder, honestly labeled — including which rungs exist and which are still being earned.

01

Your data improves your life LIVE NOW

Habits, experiments, and check-ins — insight belongs to you from day one, and the habit of constructive data collection begins.

02

A consented data commons NEXT — OPT-IN ONLY

Anonymized by default, always your call: contribute patterns to a commons and get better insights and benchmarks back. Nothing is ever taken; everything is opt-in.

03

Machines start paying people THE OPEN QUESTION

Companies and AIs that want to learn from the commons must pay the people it came from — in EVE, the network's currency. Whether institutions will actually pay enough is the single most decisive open question in this whole project. A field experiment is designed, with failure defined in advance. We'd rather test it than promise it.

04

The safety net — published, not promised A BLUEPRINT ON THE SHELF

The most heavily tested piece of the design is a floor under everyone's essentials — reserve mechanics, activation by formula, an exit that strands no one. Under harder testing it didn't fund itself, so we don't promise it. We publish it — for any community or government to fund and run in public, about a quarter cheaper than traditional delivery in modeling. If it ever activates, you'll have watched the reserve fill on a public ledger first.

One promise, made early and kept forever

EQ Coins will never convert into EVE. Your in-app coins are yours — a personal reward system, not a speculative asset. If the EVE economy arrives, it will be earned through real contribution and real data, never through grinding an app. Any project that promises otherwise is building the very thing this one exists to end. What carries forward from EdenQuest isn't coins — it's your data, your habits of self-knowledge, and your head start in a system built around them. Why this protects you →
